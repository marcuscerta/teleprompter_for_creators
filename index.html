Teleprompter glass

Turn on Mirror for a beam-splitter teleprompter like the Neewer X11 so the text reads correctly in the glass. You can also toggle it live from the on-screen controls.

Camera & recording

Shows your camera behind the script so you can see yourself while reading. Record saves the camera and microphone only (not the text). The preview is mirrored like a selfie, but the saved video is not. Safari asks for camera and microphone permission the first time. Recording quality in a web app is below the built-in Camera app.

Bluetooth remote (Satechi R2)

Pair the remote in iPad Settings → Bluetooth. Tap Learn, then press the button on your remote you want for that action. Anything it sends as a normal keyboard key works.
